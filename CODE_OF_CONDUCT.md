# Code of conduct

These rules apply in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of the octobit-dev organization.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so everyone can follow them.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Comments that demean people for who they are.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

To report a comment, issue or pull request, open its `...` menu and choose **Report content**.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the organization. The response depends on the problem: from a warning to a permanent ban.
